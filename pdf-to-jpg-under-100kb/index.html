Convert a PDF to JPG under 100KB per page

Some portals accept images only and cap each file at 100KB. This tool lowers quality and resolution until each page JPG is under that limit, or as close as the page content allows.

    How to do it

    1. Choose the PDF.
    2. Confirm the target is 100 KB.
    3. Press Convert pages and wait while each page is tried at several qualities.
    4. Download the JPGs and check they open clearly before uploading.

    Tips and limits

    • Dense photo pages may stay slightly over 100KB even at low quality — crop or extract first.
    • Greyscale scans convert smaller than colour photos.

    Frequently asked questions

    Why is one page still over 100KB?

    A full-page colour photo has a hard lower size bound. Try extracting and cropping that page, or use PNG only if the portal allows it.

    Is my file uploaded anywhere?

    No. The tool runs in your browser with JavaScript. The PDF is read from your device, processed in memory and saved back to your device. You can switch off Wi-Fi after the page loads and it still works.
