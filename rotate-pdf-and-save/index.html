Rotate a PDF and save it

Viewer rotation only lasts until you close the file. This tool saves the rotation into the PDF so it opens the right way for everyone.

Drop a file here or

    🔒 Runs entirely in your browser. Files are never uploaded.

    How to do it

    1. Choose the PDF.
    2. Pick 90° clockwise, 180° or 90° anticlockwise.
    3. Leave pages empty for all, or type e.g. 2,4.
    4. Press Rotate and download.

    Tips and limits

    • Rotation is lossless; nothing is recompressed.

    Frequently asked questions

    Why does my PDF open sideways on other computers?

    Your viewer rotated it only on screen. Saving with this tool fixes it in the file.

    Is my file uploaded anywhere?

    No. The tool runs in your browser with JavaScript. The PDF is read from your device, processed in memory and saved back to your device. You can switch off Wi-Fi after the page loads and it still works.
