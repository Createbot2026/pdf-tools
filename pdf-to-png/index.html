    How to do it

    1. Choose your PDF.
    2. High quality is on by default for sharp edges.
    3. Press Convert pages.
    4. Download each PNG.

    Tips and limits

    • PNG files are usually larger than JPG. If a portal has a tight size limit, use PDF to JPG under 100KB instead.
    • Transparent backgrounds are not created — pages get a white backdrop.

    Frequently asked questions

    JPG or PNG?

    JPG for photos and most uploads; PNG for diagrams, screenshots and when you need the sharpest text.

    Is my file uploaded anywhere?

    No. The tool runs in your browser with JavaScript. The PDF is read from your device, processed in memory and saved back to your device. You can switch off Wi-Fi after the page loads and it still works.
