Compress a PDF to under 300KB

300KB sits between the very tight 200KB caps and the more forgiving 500KB ones. It suits short scanned letters, ID packs and single certificates on older UK portals.

    🔒 Runs entirely in your browser. Files are never uploaded.

    How to do it

    1. Choose your PDF.
    2. Keep the 300 KB target; tick greyscale for black-and-white scans.
    3. Press Compress.
    4. Download and check readability at 100% zoom.

    Tips and limits

    • Three to five clean A4 scans usually fit. Longer packs need extract-first.
    • Combine related certificates, then compress once.

    Frequently asked questions

    300KB vs 200KB?

    Use whichever limit the form states. Aim a little under to be safe.

    Is my file uploaded anywhere?

    No. The tool runs in your browser with JavaScript. The PDF is read from your device, processed in memory and saved back to your device. You can switch off Wi-Fi after the page loads and it still works.
