    Frequently asked questions

    Is there a limit on how many files I can merge?

    No set limit. Very large jobs are limited only by your device’s memory.

    Does it keep the original quality?

    Yes. Merging copies pages exactly; nothing is recompressed.

    Is my file uploaded anywhere?

    No. The tool runs in your browser with JavaScript. The PDF is read from your device, processed in memory and saved back to your device. You can switch off Wi-Fi after the page loads and it still works.
