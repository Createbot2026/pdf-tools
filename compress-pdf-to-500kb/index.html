    How to do it

    1. Choose the PDF.
    2. Keep the 500 KB target.
    3. Press Compress. If the file is already small enough after a lossless re-save, you keep full quality.
    4. Download the compressed copy.

    Frequently asked questions

    Does it add a watermark?

    No. There is no watermark, no page limit and no account.

    Will my links still work?

    If the lossless re-save gets under the target, yes. If the tool has to rasterise pages, links and selectable text are lost.

    Is my file uploaded anywhere?

    No. The tool runs in your browser with JavaScript. The PDF is read from your device, processed in memory and saved back to your device. You can switch off Wi-Fi after the page loads and it still works.
