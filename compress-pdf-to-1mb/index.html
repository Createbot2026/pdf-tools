1MB is a common limit for university portals, insurance claims and supporting-evidence uploads. Most documents up to about 20 pages can get under it with good quality.

    How to do it

    1. Pick your PDF.
    2. The target is set to 1 MB (1024 KB).
    3. Press Compress.
    4. Download and compare it with the original.

    Tips and limits

    • For long scanned reports, greyscale keeps size down without hurting legibility.
    • Merging several files and then compressing once gives a smaller result than compressing each and merging.

    Frequently asked questions

    How many pages fit in 1MB?

    For typical A4 scans at readable quality, roughly 10–25 pages. Text-only PDFs can be hundreds of pages.

    Is my file uploaded anywhere?

    No. The tool runs in your browser with JavaScript. The PDF is read from your device, processed in memory and saved back to your device. You can switch off Wi-Fi after the page loads and it still works.
