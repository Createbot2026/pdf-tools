Merge PDFs on a Chromebook

ChromeOS has no built-in "combine PDFs" option, and school or work Chromebooks often block installing apps or extensions. A browser tool that runs locally is the simplest fix.

Drop files here or

    🔒 Runs entirely in your browser. Files are never uploaded.

    How to do it

    1. Open this page in Chrome on your Chromebook.
    2. Click Choose files and select PDFs from Files (Downloads, Google Drive or a USB stick).
    3. Order them with ↑, then press Merge.
    4. The merged file saves to Downloads.

    Tips and limits

    • To pick files from Google Drive, they must be available in the Files app (Drive appears there by default).
    • Hold Ctrl to select several files at once.

    Frequently asked questions

    Can I do this without Google Drive?

    Yes. Files on the Chromebook or a USB stick work the same way.

    Does it work on a school Chromebook?

    Usually yes, since it is just a web page, unless your school blocks the site.

    Is my file uploaded anywhere?

    No. The tool runs in your browser with JavaScript. The PDF is read from your device, processed in memory and saved back to your device. You can switch off Wi-Fi after the page loads and it still works.
