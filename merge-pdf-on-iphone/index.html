Merge PDFs on an iPhone

The Files app can create a PDF from images but merging several PDFs is awkward. This page merges them in Safari: pick from Files, order, download.

Drop files here or

    🔒 Runs entirely in your browser. Files are never uploaded.

    How to do it

    1. Open this page in Safari on your iPhone.
    2. Choose two or more PDFs from Files.
    3. Order with ↑ and press Merge.
    4. Save the download to Files or share it.

    Tips and limits

    • Use Compress afterwards if Mail rejects the attachment size.
    • Works on iPad the same way.

    Frequently asked questions

    Does it work in Chrome on iPhone?

    Yes, any modern iOS browser.

    Is my file uploaded anywhere?

    No. The tool runs in your browser with JavaScript. The PDF is read from your device, processed in memory and saved back to your device. You can switch off Wi-Fi after the page loads and it still works.
