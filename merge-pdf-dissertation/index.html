    How to do it

    1. Export each part to PDF.
    2. Choose them here in order: title page, abstract, contents, chapters, references, appendices.
    3. Merge and download.
    4. Check page count and size limits (Turnitin commonly accepts files up to around 100MB).

    Tips and limits

    • Merging keeps text selectable, which matters for plagiarism checks. Avoid compressing if text must stay searchable.

    Frequently asked questions

    Will Turnitin read the merged file?

    Yes. Merging does not rasterise text.

    Is my file uploaded anywhere?

    No. The tool runs in your browser with JavaScript. The PDF is read from your device, processed in memory and saved back to your device. You can switch off Wi-Fi after the page loads and it still works.
