Sign a PDF on an iPhone

You can Markup a PDF in Files, but many people prefer a dedicated pad. This page works in Safari on iPhone: draw with your finger, pick the page, and save the signed PDF to Files or Mail.

    🔒 Runs entirely in your browser. Files are never uploaded.

    How to do it

    1. Open this page in Safari on your iPhone.
    2. Choose the PDF from Files, Downloads or iCloud.
    3. Draw your signature with your finger (rotate to landscape for more room).
    4. Place it, press Sign PDF, then share or save the download.

    Tips and limits

    • If Safari blocks the download, tap the download icon in the address bar.
    • For Apple Pencil on iPad the same page works in Safari or Chrome.
    • Lock the screen orientation if the pad jumps while you draw.

    Frequently asked questions

    Does it work offline on iPhone?

    After the page has loaded once, turn on Airplane Mode and it still runs.

    What about HEIC photos of a signed paper form?

    Photograph the form, convert with HEIC to PDF, then merge if needed.

    Is my file uploaded anywhere?

    No. The tool runs in your browser with JavaScript. The PDF is read from your device, processed in memory and saved back to your device. You can switch off Wi-Fi after the page loads and it still works.
