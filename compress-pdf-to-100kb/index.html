Compress a PDF to under 100KB

A 100KB cap is typical for ID proofs, signatures and single-page certificates on government and exam portals. It is realistic for one or two pages; beyond that, expect to use greyscale.

    How to do it

    1. Select the PDF.
    2. Keep the 100 KB target and tick greyscale if the original is a black-and-white scan.
    3. Press Compress and wait a few seconds per page.
    4. Download and check the file size shown on the button is under 100KB.

    Tips and limits

    • Crop your scan to the document edges before making the PDF; white margins still cost bytes.
    • Photos of documents taken on a phone are often 3–4MB per page. Use the images to PDF tool, then compress.
    • If a portal says "100KB" it may mean 100,000 bytes, so aim for 95KB to be safe.

    Frequently asked questions

    Is 100KB the same as 0.1MB?

    Roughly. Some sites use 1KB = 1,000 bytes and others 1,024. Aim a little under the limit to be safe.

    Can I compress a 20-page PDF to 100KB?

    Usually not legibly. Extract only the pages the form asks for, then compress.

    Is my file uploaded anywhere?

    No. The tool runs in your browser with JavaScript. The PDF is read from your device, processed in memory and saved back to your device. You can switch off Wi-Fi after the page loads and it still works.
