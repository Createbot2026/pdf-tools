Merge bank statements into one PDF

Mortgage brokers, letting agents and visa applications commonly ask for three to six months of statements, often as one file. Bank statements are exactly the documents you should not upload to unknown websites.

Drop files here or

    🔒 Runs entirely in your browser. Files are never uploaded.

    How to do it

    1. Download each month’s statement from your banking app as PDF.
    2. Choose them all here and order oldest to newest.
    3. Press Merge.
    4. If the result is too big, run it through the compress tool.

    Tips and limits

    • Check each statement shows your name and address if the recipient needs proof of address.
    • Do not edit statements: lenders check for alterations.

    Frequently asked questions

    Is it safe?

    The files are combined inside your browser. Nothing is sent to a server.

    Is my file uploaded anywhere?

    No. The tool runs in your browser with JavaScript. The PDF is read from your device, processed in memory and saved back to your device. You can switch off Wi-Fi after the page loads and it still works.
