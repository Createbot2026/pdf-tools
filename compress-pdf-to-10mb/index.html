Compress a PDF to under 10MB for email

Many US and workplace mail servers still struggle above 10MB, and some cap closer to 4MB. This page defaults to 10MB; type 4096 in the target box for a 4MB cap.

    How to do it

    1. Choose the large PDF.
    2. Keep 10 MB, or type 4096 for a 4MB limit.
    3. Press Compress.
    4. Attach the downloaded file to your email.

    Tips and limits

    • Gmail and Outlook.com allow larger attachments via Drive/OneDrive links, but a 10MB file still sends cleanly as a normal attachment in most places.
    • For huge scans, split into parts as a backup plan.

    Frequently asked questions

    10MB or 4MB?

    Use the limit your recipient or mail server states. 10MB is the safer default for personal email; 4MB suits stricter corporate gateways.

    Is my file uploaded anywhere?

    No. The tool runs in your browser with JavaScript. The PDF is read from your device, processed in memory and saved back to your device. You can switch off Wi-Fi after the page loads and it still works.
