    How to do it

    1. Photograph each document flat, in good light.
    2. Choose the HEIC files here and order them.
    3. Convert to PDF (A4).
    4. Run the result through Compress PDF to match the portal’s KB or MB limit.

    Tips and limits

    • Check the portal’s stated limit (often 2MB). Use the matching compress page.
    • Delete photos from Camera Roll and iCloud afterwards if you prefer.
    • One document per photo keeps caseworker review easier.

    Frequently asked questions

    Will UKVI accept a phone photo PDF?

    Clear, complete page photos are commonly accepted, but follow the instructions on your upload page.

    Is my file uploaded anywhere?

    No. The tool runs in your browser with JavaScript. The PDF is read from your device, processed in memory and saved back to your device. You can switch off Wi-Fi after the page loads and it still works.
