Split a large PDF into smaller parts

When compression would make a document unreadable, splitting it is the better option. Many portals allow multiple attachments even when each is size-limited.

Drop a file here or

    🔒 Runs entirely in your browser. Files are never uploaded.

    How to do it

    1. Check the total page count.
    2. Type ranges, e.g. 1-20;21-40;41-.
    3. Press Split and upload each part, naming them "Part 1 of 3" etc.

    Tips and limits

    • Split at natural breaks like section starts so each part makes sense alone.

    Does splitting reduce quality?

    No. Pages are copied exactly.

    No. The tool runs in your browser with JavaScript. The PDF is read from your device, processed in memory and saved back to your device. You can switch off Wi-Fi after the page loads and it still works.
