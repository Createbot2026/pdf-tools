Convert HEIC photos to PDF

iPhones save photos as HEIC by default. Many Windows PCs and upload portals do not open HEIC. This tool converts HEIC (and HEIF) to JPEG in your browser, then builds a PDF you can email or upload.

    🔒 Runs entirely in your browser. Files are never uploaded.

    How to do it

    1. Choose one or more HEIC photos (JPG/PNG also accepted).
    2. Pick A4 pages or fit-to-image.
    3. Press Convert to PDF.
    4. Download heic.pdf.

    Tips and limits

    • On iPhone you can also set Camera → Formats → Most Compatible to save JPG going forward.
    • Large Live Photos only use the still frame.
    • Convert then compress if an upload limit applies.

    Frequently asked questions

    Does it work on Windows?

    Yes. That is the main reason people need it — Windows often cannot open HEIC without extra codecs.

    Is metadata kept?

    No. The conversion focuses on a viewable image; EXIF is not copied.

    Is my file uploaded anywhere?

    No. The tool runs in your browser with JavaScript. The PDF is read from your device, processed in memory and saved back to your device. You can switch off Wi-Fi after the page loads and it still works.
