Photographing your passport on a phone and converting it locally avoids leaving copies of your ID on conversion sites.

    🔒 Runs entirely in your browser. Files are never uploaded.

    How to do it

    1. Photograph the photo page flat, in good light, with no glare.
    2. Choose the image here, select A4.
    3. Create the PDF, then compress if there is a size limit.

    Tips and limits

    • Include all four corners of the page; cropped scans may be rejected.
    • Delete the photo from your camera roll and cloud backup afterwards if you prefer.

    Frequently asked questions

    Is a phone photo acceptable?

    Many online checks accept clear photos, but follow the specific instructions you were given.

    Is my file uploaded anywhere?

    No. The tool runs in your browser with JavaScript. The PDF is read from your device, processed in memory and saved back to your device. You can switch off Wi-Fi after the page loads and it still works.
