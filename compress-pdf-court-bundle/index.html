Compress a court or tribunal bundle PDF

Bundles for employment tribunals and county court claims often run to hundreds of pages and quickly exceed email or e-filing limits. Compression helps, but splitting into volumes is sometimes required anyway.

    How to do it

    1. Merge all documents in the agreed order first.
    2. Compress the merged bundle (leave target blank for balanced quality, or set one).
    3. If still too large, use the split tool to create Volume 1, Volume 2 and so on.

    Tips and limits

    • Rasterising removes selectable text and bookmarks. If the court wants searchable bundles, only use the lossless result or split instead.
    • Always check page numbering matches the index after any change.

    Frequently asked questions

    Will compression break my bundle’s page numbers?

    No pages are added or removed, so numbering stays the same.

    Is my file uploaded anywhere?

    No. The tool runs in your browser with JavaScript. The PDF is read from your device, processed in memory and saved back to your device. You can switch off Wi-Fi after the page loads and it still works.
