Split a PDF into single pages

Useful for separating scanned batches, payslips stored in one file, or individual invoices.

    🔒 Runs entirely in your browser. Files are never uploaded.

    How to do it

    1. Choose the PDF.
    2. Leave the page box empty to split every page.
    3. Press Split; one download link appears per page.

    Tips and limits

    • Want groups instead? Type ranges separated by semicolons, e.g. 1-3;4-6.

    Frequently asked questions

    How do I split into groups?

    Type groups like 1-2;3-5;6- (the last means page 6 to the end).

    Is my file uploaded anywhere?

    No. The tool runs in your browser with JavaScript. The PDF is read from your device, processed in memory and saved back to your device. You can switch off Wi-Fi after the page loads and it still works.
