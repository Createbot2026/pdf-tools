Fill a PDF form online

Government, HR and school forms often arrive as fillable PDFs. This tool reads the form fields on your device, lets you type into them, and saves a completed copy — optionally flattened so the answers cannot be edited later.

Drop a file here or

    🔒 Runs entirely in your browser. Files are never uploaded.

    How to do it

    1. Choose the fillable PDF.
    2. The fields appear below; type into each one (tick boxes work too).
    3. Optionally tick Flatten to lock the answers.
    4. Press Fill form and download.

    Tips and limits

    • If no fields appear, the PDF is a flat scan — use Sign PDF to add a signature image, or add text with a PDF editor.
    • Flatten before emailing if you do not want recipients to change your answers.
    • Dropdowns and radio groups are supported when the form defines them.

    Frequently asked questions

    Why are there no fields?

    The file has no AcroForm fields. Scanned paper forms need signing or a fresh digital form.

    Are XFA forms supported?

    No. Older Adobe XFA forms need Acrobat. Most modern UK forms use AcroForm.

    Is my file uploaded anywhere?

    No. The tool runs in your browser with JavaScript. The PDF is read from your device, processed in memory and saved back to your device. You can switch off Wi-Fi after the page loads and it still works.
