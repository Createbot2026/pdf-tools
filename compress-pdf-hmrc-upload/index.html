Compress a PDF to upload to HMRC

HMRC sometimes asks you to send supporting evidence through an online form, such as receipts, P60s or letters. Tax paperwork contains your National Insurance number and income, so compressing it locally is sensible.

    🔒 Runs entirely in your browser. Files are never uploaded.

    How to do it

    1. Scan your receipts or letters to PDF.
    2. Merge them into one file if appropriate.
    3. Compress with the default 2MB target, or the limit the HMRC page states.
    4. Upload the compressed copy.

    Tips and limits

    • Group receipts by tax year to keep evidence tidy.
    • Greyscale is ideal for till receipts and letters.

    Frequently asked questions

    What file types does HMRC accept?

    PDF is widely accepted on HMRC upload forms, but check the specific form’s guidance.

    Is my file uploaded anywhere?

    No. The tool runs in your browser with JavaScript. The PDF is read from your device, processed in memory and saved back to your device. You can switch off Wi-Fi after the page loads and it still works.
