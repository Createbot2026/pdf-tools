    How to do it

    1. Choose the PDF.
    2. Draw on the signature pad, or type your name in the box.
    3. Pick the page and where to place it (bottom right is usual).
    4. Press Sign PDF and download the signed copy.

    Tips and limits

    • This is a visual signature image, not a cryptographic digital certificate. For legally binding e-sign platforms, follow the instructions you were given.
    • Zoom the downloaded file to check placement before you send it.
    • Clear the pad and redraw if the first attempt wobbles.

    Frequently asked questions

    Is a drawn signature legally valid?

    In the UK a simple electronic signature is often enough for everyday contracts, but some documents need a qualified digital signature or a wet-ink original. Check what the recipient requires.

    Can I upload a signature image?

    Draw or type here. For a saved PNG signature, the Private PDF Toolkit extension will support it when it launches.

    Is my file uploaded anywhere?

    No. The tool runs in your browser with JavaScript. The PDF is read from your device, processed in memory and saved back to your device. You can switch off Wi-Fi after the page loads and it still works.
