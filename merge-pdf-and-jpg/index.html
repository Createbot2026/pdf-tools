Merge PDFs and JPG images into one file

Application packs often mix digital PDFs (offers, contracts) with phone photos (receipts, ID). This tool accepts both in one list and builds one ordered PDF.

Drop files here or

    🔒 Runs entirely in your browser. Files are never uploaded.

    How to do it

    1. Choose PDFs and JPG/PNG images together.
    2. Order with ↑ (images become pages; PDFs keep theirs).
    3. Pick A4 or fit-to-image for the photo pages.
    4. Press Merge files and download.

    Tips and limits

    • Compress afterwards if the combined file exceeds an upload limit.
    • Name downloads clearly before you attach them.

    Frequently asked questions

    Does merging recompress my PDFs?

    No. PDF pages are copied exactly. Only images are embedded fresh.

    Is my file uploaded anywhere?

    No. The tool runs in your browser with JavaScript. The PDF is read from your device, processed in memory and saved back to your device. You can switch off Wi-Fi after the page loads and it still works.
